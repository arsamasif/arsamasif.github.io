QC render and turntables

Problems with assets and caches are cheapest to fix before they reach a shot. This system rendered every published asset and cache under a standard light rig automatically, and gave artists a turntable tool for looking at their work in context, so supervisors could approve what they had actually seen.

What I built

Renderer-agnostic core

  • A base class that defines every step of a QC render, with renderer-specific subclasses for Arnold and V-Ray.
  • The renderer chosen from configuration, so a show could switch without code changes.
  • Render layers, collections and overrides built through Render Setup.

Scene assembly

  • Automatic discovery of the right caches, with scoring to pick the best match.
  • Lookup of published shaders and the light rig for the asset or shot.
  • USD references brought in as renderer standins.
  • Cameras found through tags rather than naming conventions.

Farm and tracking

  • Render farm jobs built and submitted with their plug-in settings.
  • Versions and tasks created in production tracking for every render.
  • Render elements and EXR metadata written for compositing and review.
  • An option to submit QC renders automatically as part of publishing.

Turntable tool

  • HDRI lighting, backdrop and camera setup in one click.
  • Shading modes for wireframe, UV checker, ambient occlusion and displacement.
  • AOV selection and a camera keyframe editor.
  • Settings saved and loaded so turntables matched across a team.
